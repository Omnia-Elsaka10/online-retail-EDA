# Online Retail - Exploratory Data Analysis

## Project Overview
A comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis of the
Online Retail dataset to uncover customer behavior,
product performance, sales trends, and geographical patterns.

## Dataset
- Source: UCI Machine Learning Repository
- Records: 541,909
- Features: 6

## Objectives
- Analyze customer behavior and spending patterns.
- Identify top-selling and low-selling products.
- Explore monthly and daily sales trends.
- Compare sales activity across countries.
- Investigate unit price distributions and unusual values.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab

## Key Findings
- The United Kingdom accounts for 91.43% of records.
- The Netherlands ranks second in total quantity sold.
- November 2011 recorded the highest monthly revenue.
- PAPER CRAFT, LITTLE BIRDIE ranked first by quantity sold.
- Unit prices are strongly right-skewed.
